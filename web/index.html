dissolve

dissolve (npm: dissolve-ui) is an open source animation library for React Native, with 36 animated UI components for React Native and React. Components are copied into your app with npx dissolve-ui add <name> or the shadcn CLI: the source is yours to edit, styled with classNames and the --dissolve-* theme tokens.

The React Native engine turns any view into dust: the view is snapshotted with Skia makeImageFromView, cut into grains drawn with one <Atlas>, and animated on the UI thread with Reanimated 4. Grain counts are capped at 6000 per burst. It works on iOS and Android, and in Expo Go.

On the web, each component is a React component with the same props. It needs only React 19 and react-dom, draws on a canvas, and works in the Next.js App Router and Vite.

Get started

React Native: install the engine and its peers, wrap the app once in <DissolveProvider>, then add components.

React: no engine and no provider, one command per component.

Step by step: Installation. With a coding agent: Agent prompt.

Components

  • SwipeToDelete: Swipe a row left to delete it: it dissolves, then the list closes up.
  • Toast: Toasts that arrive in grains and leave as dust, with an optional Undo action.
  • GlossyButton: Tactile pill lit from the inside: gradient stroke, inner light and ring, presses down on a crisp spring.
  • LikeButton: Heart that pops with sparks on like and snaps into grains on unlike.
  • MorphButton: Dynamic Island style pill that folds into a loader, then opens as the done state.
  • SplitButton: Splits in two like a drop of liquid metal, Cancel and Confirm halves.
  • GooeyMenu: Floating button whose actions pour out as gooey drops.
  • SlideToConfirm: Slide to pay or confirm, shimmer label, loader, done state.
  • OTPInput: One time code boxes, verify sweep, success tint, shake and dust on reject.
  • NumberTicker: Rolling digit columns for prices and counters, locale aware.
  • ReactionPicker: iMessage style reactions: long press, dock magnifier, emoji flies to a badge.
  • VoiceNote: Voice message bubble: play, scrub the waveform with ticks, unsend as dust.
  • SignaturePad: Variable width Skia signature, undo, clear into dust, PNG export.
  • CardStack: Wallet style card stack: tap to focus, drag down to put back, swipe to cycle.
  • BeforeAfter: Before / after image comparison with a draggable divider and haptic ticks.
  • ThinkingOrb: A sphere of dots that shows what an AI agent is doing: thinking, searching, listening, done.
  • ImageReveal: Image generation loader: a thinking mosaic that resolves coarse to fine, then settles in grains.
  • SweepTransition: A crisp liquid band sweeps across a view and leaves the new state behind it.
  • UploadTile: Attachment that assembles from grains, fills a progress ring, rejected files leave as dust.
  • TaskCheck: Checkbox that draws its check and strikes the label, then the task moves away or dissolves.
  • QuantityStepper: Minus and plus with rolling digits and an accelerating long press; at 1 it turns into a trash.
  • RulerPicker: Horizontal ruler with momentum, rolling value and a haptic tick per graduation.
  • BarChart: Bar chart you scrub with a finger, sliding bubble, bars regrow on period change.
  • MiniPlayer: Now playing pill that opens into a full player as one object, then folds back.
  • PullToRefresh: Pull a list down: grains gather into a loader, release and the content rebuilds itself.
  • ReorderList: Long press a row to lift it, drag to reorder, the others make room, it settles with a tick.
  • ScratchCard: Glossy foil scratch card that tilts in 3D under the finger, sheds grains, then dissolves past the threshold.
  • Skeleton: Loading placeholders that break into grains and recompose into the real content, block by block.
  • SegmentedControl: iOS segmented control whose pill breaks into grains, flows to the new segment and reassembles. Drag across to follow the finger.
  • Toggle: iOS switch whose thumb breaks into grains, flows to the other end of the track and reassembles while the track fills.
  • ProgressSteps: Numbered steps on a line, or story style bars: the progress streams on to the next step in grains.
  • ElasticSlider: Thick rounded slider whose texts invert over the fill; pulled past an end, the track stretches like a rubber band and snaps back.
  • Accordion: Expandable sections: the height glides open, the chevron turns over and the content slides in.
  • CopyButton: Icon button that copies text: the copy icon breaks into grains that reform as a check, a Copied tooltip rises.
  • AvatarStack: Overlapping avatars with a +N chip: hover or hold to spread them with names, adding or removing one animates the stack.
  • Tooltip: A label with an arrow on hover, focus or long press: it grows in, flips and slides to stay on screen.

Effects

Exits

  • Dust: Any view snapshots into grains that drift away on the wind, left edge first.
  • Sand: Grains pour straight down with gravity and slide out of frame.
  • Ember: Burns from the edge and rises as sparks tinted by the view itself.
  • Pixel: A blocky, stepped collapse for retro and game UIs.
  • Vortex: Grains spiral into the center and vanish.
  • Shatter: Glass shards break away from the point of impact.
  • Stream: The view flies into a target, like a file dropped in the trash.

Entrances

  • Gather: Grains converge from a loose cloud into the view.
  • Rain: Grains fall and settle, bottom row first.
  • Print: The view is printed in, line by line.

Gestures

  • Snap: Bursts outward from the exact point you touched.
  • Fling: Follows the velocity of the swipe that threw it away.
  • Charge: Hold to heat the view up, it bursts at full charge.

Special

  • Morph: View A breaks down and rebuilds itself as view B.

Casual

  • Peel: Peels off from the corner like a sticker, then flies away.
  • Melt: Melts down in drips, like wax under a lamp.
